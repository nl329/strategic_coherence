About this measure

Uses GPT-5.6 with high reasoning effort for alignment, logical reasoning, and convincing reasoning. Alignment uses the latest objective-based notebook prompt; the logical and convincing prompts are unchanged. Topic is metadata only. Logical scores range from illogical (1) to logical (5). Convincing scores range from reducing belief in company success (1), through no change (3), to increasing belief (5). These are model estimates, not validated predictions of success.
